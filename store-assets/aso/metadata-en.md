Golem Forge is a real-time strategy game of castle war. Two alchemists forge golem armies and send them down the same battle corridor. Your goal: crush the enemy castle in a live 1v1 match.

BUILD YOUR ARMY
Train melee, ranged and heavy golems. They march and fight on their own; you decide when the army attacks and when it falls back to hold. Mind the power triangle: melee beats ranged, ranged beats heavy, heavy beats melee, each for 1.5× damage. Read your rival's army and answer with the right golems.

DEFEND YOUR CASTLE
Build turrets on your castle. Every age brings its own Light, Medium and Heavy turret: Arrow Tower, Pinecone Mangonel, Fire Cauldron, Prism Tower, Aether Crystal and more. Love tower defense? Here your turrets hold the line while your army fights up front.

5 AGES, 15 GOLEMS

AGE CARDS
Every time you advance an age, you are dealt 3 cards. Keep one, and its effect lasts the whole match. Army, economy, defense or power: a pool of 15 cards keeps every battle different.

OUTPOSTS AND THE AETHER WELL
Capture the 3 points along the corridor (+25% income each) and build outposts so your golems spawn closer to the front. When the Aether Well appears near the middle, the side that holds it alone claims gold, Aether Fury or a full heal.

COMMANDER AND SPECIAL POWERS
Your commander joins every match, leaps into the enemy line with Quake Slam and levels up with every kill. Each age has its own special power that rains down on the enemy front line: Pinecone Rain, Molten Bronze, Cannonball Rain, Crystal Shower, Aether Storm.

PLAY ONLINE
• Quick Match: we look for another player; if nobody turns up after a short wait, an AI opponent that plays like a person steps in
• Create Room: share a 4-letter code and battle a friend
• Phone and browser players meet in the same match
• Play vs Bot on Easy, Normal or Hard
• Ask for a rematch when the battle ends
An internet connection is required for every mode.

GUIDED TUTORIAL
A 3–4 minute guided match teaches you the game step by step. New to strategy games? Start here. In real matches, a short tip pops up the first time something new happens.

6 LANGUAGES
Play in English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Español, Français or Português. The game follows your device language, and you can switch it in the menu.

No endless stalemates: after minute 15 the Doom Clock starts, and castles slowly crumble.

March your golems, hold the line, crush their castle!

## Keyword rationale

- **Name "Golem Forge: Castle War" (23).** The brand matches the in-game logo "GOLEM FORGE". "Castle" and "war" are high-volume genre words; Apple combines them with subtitle and keyword terms ("castle defense", "war strategy", "army battle").
- **Subtitle "PvP Army Strategy & Defense" (27).** "Strategy" and "defense" are the most-tracked genre terms in this space; "army" names the core loop; "PvP" is backed by rooms with friends and quick-match players (the copy says plainly that an AI opponent may fill in).
- **Keywords.** `tower` + `td` (the castle turrets are real gameplay, but the subtitle does not promise a pure tower-defense game), `battle`, `rts`, `lane`, `siege`, `multiplayer`, `online`, `friend`, `1v1` (matches are exactly two sides), `troop`, `commander`, `fantasy`, `duel`, `evolution` (5 ages). Nothing repeats the name/subtitle (golem, forge, castle, war, pvp, army, strategy, defense); no plural pairs.
- **Game vocabulary.** The copy uses the English UI's own words: Age / age card, Aether, Aether Well, outposts, turrets, power triangle, Quake Slam, Doom Clock, Quick Match, Create Room, Play vs Bot. "Age" stays out of the keyword field (too close to other titles), but it is the game's word, so the copy uses it.
- **Left out on purpose:** price and ad claims, offline play (bot matches need the server), rankings, awards, player counts, other games' names.
- Re-tune after launch with App Store Connect / Play Console search-term data.
